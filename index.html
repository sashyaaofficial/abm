Approach

How I run an account-focused program

Every program I run follows the same six steps. Each one below comes with an example from work I've delivered.

i.

Define the accounts and buying group

Start with who has to be in the room: the companies, the roles that decide, and the roles that influence.

CQTA programs target quality engineering and testing leaders at enterprise banks, telecoms and insurers.
ii.

Agree the goals and plays with stakeholders

Align on success criteria, personas and the play for each segment before anything is designed.

Plans set with CQTA organizers, speakers, sponsors and venue teams for each quarterly program.
iii.

Orchestrate the channels

Events, paid social, email and organic content, sequenced so each touch hands people to the next.

Summer Mega Event: 50+ posts, a four-week LinkedIn campaign and a full Brevo email suite.
iv.

Personalize by segment

Past attendees, new prospects, speakers and sponsors each get a message written for them.

Separate sponsor, co-marketing, VIP and attendee emails for the same event.
v.

Capture and route engagement

Registrations, opens and clicks are tracked, segmented and passed on for timely follow-up.

Eventbrite registrations flow into Brevo segments that shape the next send.
vi.

Measure, report and standardize

Close every program with results and lessons, then turn what worked into a template.

Post-event reports, plus a reusable playbook each new CQTA event launches from.

The details that keep a program on track

Timelines, approvals, vendors, budgets and follow-up. This is what makes a program repeatable, and it's the part I enjoy.

1

Timelines and approvals

Content calendars and countdown schedules for each program. I'm the post approver on client channels, so deliverables ship on time and on brand.

2

Vendor, venue and partner coordination

Venues, sponsors, speakers, photographers and co-marketing partners, from booking dates through to the day itself.

3

Budget tracking

Daily caps, spend per campaign and cost per result tracked and reported, so every dollar can be accounted for.

4

Lists and deliverability

Prospect lists built in Sales Navigator, validated and cleaned before sending, then split into segments by role and relationship.

5

Thank-you emails, feedback forms, sponsor and speaker notes, and a post-program report with results and next steps.
