Proposed plan

My first 90 days

A starting draft. I'd refine it with my manager in week one.

30

Learn the accounts and the system

  • Meet Sales, Strategy and Campaign Planning partners for priority accounts
  • Learn the ABM stack, including 6sense and Workfront, and current reporting
  • Review recent programs: what was run, for whom, and what it returned
  • Take on coordination for one live program
60

Run the operations

  • Own timelines, approvals, vendors and budget tracking for assigned programs
  • Check that ABM engagement is captured and routed to the right sales channel
  • Set up a simple follow-up tracker for lead flow with Sales
  • Contribute event and email ideas for priority buying groups
90

Improve and standardize

  • Deliver a post-program readout with the insights team
  • Turn one repeat process into a template or checklist
  • Propose one test to reduce friction in the buyer journey
  • Agree next-quarter priorities with the team
